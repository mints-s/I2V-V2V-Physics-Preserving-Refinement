# Claim Safety Check

## Safe Claims

- We propose a conceptual output-side refinement structure.
- Frozen I2V backbone outputs can be treated as Stage-1 motion priors.
- Off-the-shelf V2V refinement improves sharpness but can introduce dynamics drift.
- Dynamics preservation should be part of output head design.
- The current VEnhancer sweep provides pilot evidence for the visual gain-dynamics drift trade-off.
- The proposed direction targets output refinement for frozen I2V backbones under Stage-1 dynamics preservation.

## Unsafe Claims

- We guarantee physical correctness.
- We fully preserve physics.
- We are the first to enhance generated video.
- We are the first to use frozen backbone + adapter.
- We already trained a complete new model, unless actually implemented.
- Our refinement is globally optimal.
- This proves true physical correctness.

## Must-Use Terms

- Stage-1 dynamics preservation
- physics-induced motion prior
- output refinement head
- frozen I2V backbone
- visual gain-dynamics drift trade-off

## Avoid Terms

- physical ground truth
- perfect preservation
- guaranteed physics
- universal optimality
- mere upscaling

## Recommended Framing

The paper should state that Stage-1 I2V output is a physics-induced motion prior generated by a frozen I2V backbone. The output refinement head is designed to improve visual/spatio-temporal quality while limiting drift from that prior. The current RealWonder + VEnhancer results motivate the need for this design, but they do not by themselves constitute a completed trainable head.
